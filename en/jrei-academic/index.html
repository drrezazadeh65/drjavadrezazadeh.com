Frequently asked questions

Is the platform live now?

This is a product introduction. Operational access will be announced after integration and specialist acceptance.

Should private data be submitted?

No. Do not send sensitive data for an initial enquiry. Agree scope, authorization and protection before any data is received.

Are outcomes guaranteed?

No. Acceptance criteria must be defined for the specific task and results evaluated using actual evidence.
