Understanding an educational decision before prescribing a pathway

A consequential educational choice often contains several legitimate but competing priorities. A student may be balancing motivation, available learning opportunities, family expectations, time, resources and uncertainty about future academic plans. These considerations should be distinguished from claims that a single assessment or consultation can reveal one permanently correct direction.

The proposed decision map sets out possible pathways, the reasoning behind them and practical actions that can be revisited as circumstances change. It is intended to support deliberation and learner agency, not to label ability, diagnose a condition or promise future attainment. The quality of a recommendation depends on the completeness and relevance of the evidence provided.
