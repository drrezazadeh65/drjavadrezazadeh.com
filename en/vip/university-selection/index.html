Making university choices under real constraints

Prioritising programmes is not simply a matter of ranking institutions by prestige. The applicant’s verified academic standing, eligibility rules, preferred disciplines, geographic constraints, financial considerations and longer-term interests may pull in different directions. A responsible comparison should make those tensions explicit and record the reasons for the order in which eligible options are placed.

The proposed written decision map is intended to help applicants recognise assumptions and clarify the consequences of their choices. Official admission notices and current programme rules remain authoritative; no advisory analysis can assure selection or displace the applicant’s own final decision.

The VIP overview and standard service catalogue provide alternative scopes. Only a separately confirmed agreement establishes the deliverables, timelines and any payable amount.
