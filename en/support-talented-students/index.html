From verified need to documented progress

These are operating controls for the programme, not a claim that every stage or student file is already live.

01Needs assessment

A student and their legal guardian submit a private request; trained staff check eligibility and evidence.

02Guest giving

A contributor can donate without an account. Verified payments remain distinct from bookstore orders.

03Approved allocation

Funds are allocated to verified needs only with authorised review and expense records.

04Restricted reporting

Progress and outcomes are shown according to each user's role and revocable access rights.

Choice, dignity and child safeguarding

No donor acquires a right to a child's name, photograph, school, address, telephone number or private messaging merely by contributing.

Student and guardian consent

A student may wish to know their sponsor. Limited identity information becomes available only after the sponsor also consents, the guardian provides valid authorisation, the student's age-appropriate wishes are respected and the safeguarding team approves access.

  • Minimum necessary identity disclosure
  • Revocable consent and auditable permissions
  • No addresses or direct contact details

Sponsor choice

A sponsor may remain anonymous, or request an authorised view of the student's verified needs and progress. Child and guardian consent, account verification and safeguarding review are mandatory. Requests go through staff, not directly to children.

  • No private chat, messaging or phone exchange
  • No unilateral editing of a student's declared needs
  • Explicit expiry and revocation of access

Private student and sponsor dashboards

Private features require a verified account, approved role and consent-based authorisation. Real student information remains inaccessible while these safeguards and account-email verification are incomplete.

RESPONSIBLE SPONSORSHIP GUIDE

Frequently asked questions about educational support

Clear answers about amounts, allocation, mutual consent, privacy and payment follow-up.

Do I need to register to contribute?

No. Guest contributions are available. Connecting past contributions to a private sponsor account later requires secure verification.

Can I view a student's identity?

Not automatically. A legal guardian's authorisation, the student's age-appropriate agreement, sponsor consent and staff approval are needed for any permitted limited view.

Can I contact the student directly?

No. The programme does not provide direct messaging, private calls or exchange of contact details between sponsors and children.

Is a payment receipt proof that money has already been allocated?

No. Verified payment and recorded expenditure are distinct processes; case allocation requires further checks.

What contribution limits apply?

You may enter any whole-toman amount from 1,000 to 10,000,000. Selecting one of the six suggested amounts is optional.

Can I earmark support for books or tuition?

You may indicate an area, but actual allocation needs verified requirements and documented expenditure.

Does choosing an area identify a particular student?

No. It indicates a preference only and does not create automatic access to a student's private case.

Do I need an account to see my payment history?

A guest can contribute without registration, but personal records and reports require verified email identity and access authorization.

May a sponsor remain anonymous?

Yes. Identity visibility is governed by valid consent and child-safeguarding rules. Private details are not made public.

What happens if consent for disclosure is withdrawn?

Previously permitted limited access is revoked under the consent process; required financial and audit records are retained.

What if I am charged without receiving a receipt?

Do not pay twice. Email the transaction reference, amount and date, never complete payment card details or credentials.

Can I see how a contribution was spent?

Only authorized and verified reports of needs and expenditure are accessible in the private workspace. A payment receipt does not establish that allocation has occurred.
