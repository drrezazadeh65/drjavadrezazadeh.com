Frequently asked questions

Does Forge depend on GitHub?

The intended runtime is independent of GitHub, with locally managed repositories and engines.

Can I use my own hosting?

That is a design goal. Each target requires acceptance against its actual capabilities and protocols.

Will private JREI data be hosted here?

No. The public web layer is separate from private memory, models, credentials and engines.

Can I subscribe now?

No. This is a design preview only.
