Books & Creative Work

Writing across education, research and poetry.

My writing has never belonged to a single register. Academic work asks for evidence, conceptual discipline and careful qualification; educational writing must remain usable in the lives of learners and families; poetry works through another kind of attention. I keep these forms distinct, but I do not regard them as unrelated.
