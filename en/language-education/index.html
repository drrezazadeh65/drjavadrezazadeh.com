English Language Education

Language education becomes meaningful when evidence, materials and learner experience meet.

My work in English language education sits within Applied Linguistics and connects classroom practice with questions of materials, assessment, vocabulary learning, academic literacy, technology and learner development. The emphasis is not on one method for every learner, but on making principled educational decisions in context.

Areas of work

A connected language-education programme

Language Teaching & Learning

Classroom learning, learner strategy, language development and the conditions under which instruction becomes usable beyond the lesson itself.

Materials & Curriculum

Evaluation and development of learning materials with attention to purpose, learner context, authenticity, usability and educational fit.

Assessment

Assessment as evidence for decisions rather than an isolated score, with attention to interpretation, consequences and responsible use.

Vocabulary & Academic Literacy

Vocabulary learning, academic reading and writing, and the development of language needed for sustained educational participation.

Technology & AI

Use of digital tools and AI in ways that support learning without replacing learner effort, judgement or independent performance.

Research-informed Practice

Bringing classroom evidence, scholarly literature and professional judgement together rather than treating research and teaching as separate worlds.
