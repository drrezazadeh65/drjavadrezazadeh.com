Seyyed Mehrab Mirkarimi: 36 years of questions and explanatory answers

A structured digital collection for candidates preparing for the Iranian national Master's entrance examination in English Language Teaching. The author has brought together examination questions from 1370 to 1405 in the Iranian calendar (approximately 1991–2026), alongside a separate explanatory answer volume.

The materials are described here for academic and informational purposes. A digital purchase is not offered until distribution rights, secure file custody and actual payment-to-delivery operations have been verified.

Educational resources · Evidence-aware presentation

A two-volume digital study collection

The supplied materials consist of two independent PDFs, with 997 pages in total. The compiler describes the text as having been retyped and revised for readability. The explanatory volume aims to support reflection on answer choices, rather than simply reproducing answer keys.

According to the author, more detailed bibliographic references to sources, editions and pages are included in portions covering 1390–1405. He also reports using AI tools to simplify or condense some explanations for earlier examination years. These statements are attributed to the author, not presented as independent verification of every answer.

There is no claim that all 997 pages have undergone an independent scholarly review, nor that the work is officially endorsed by the examination authority. Preparation materials do not guarantee admission or a particular examination outcome.

About the compiler

Seyyed Mehrab Mirkarimi compiled the collection in response to the practical difficulty of locating coherent, readable past examination materials. The author reports an extended process of compilation, correction and editorial revision. Any public academic ranking or institutional distinction should be supported by verifiable documentation before being described as independently confirmed.

Digital publication and rights: The materials are currently being prepared for lawful digital distribution. Publication and sale require a verified contributor licence, an approved author account, protected PDFs outside public web directories, a working delivery mechanism and a tested payment process.

Transparent educational-support allocation

Author remuneration and support for talented learners

The owner's approved domestic gross purchase price is 1,000,000 tomans. For each successfully verified sale, 600,000 tomans are assigned to the author's entitlement and 400,000 tomans are earmarked for supporting talented children and young people facing financial disadvantage. This constitutes 40% of the buyer's price, not a claim that 40% of an undefined net profit has already been spent.

A separate 70,000-toman platform-cost reserve, if established and funded, is accounted for independently; it must not be deducted from either allocation. Earmarking support is not the same as a completed disbursement to beneficiaries.
