Purchase and order tracking guide

A clear path from selection to order tracking, with guidance on verified payments, printed-book delivery and specialist services.

Before you pay

Review the book or service description, stated price, scope and exclusions. Checkout is available only when the relevant payment category is enabled. A return from a bank gateway is not by itself proof of successful payment; the transaction must be verified by the payment system.

Printed books

Physical orders require recipient and delivery details, including address and postal code. Postal shipping is free for book orders. Estimated delivery is 10–14 working days after order confirmation and stock verification; actual timing may vary by destination and carrier conditions.

Academic and VIP services

Service fees cover the stated professional scope and do not guarantee examination results, admission or employment. Session scheduling and any agreed deliverables are coordinated by email after order confirmation.

Commissioning a professional or VIP service: six careful steps

Choosing a service, making an enquiry, verifying payment and confirming an appointment are separate events. None should be inferred merely from the preceding step.

  1. Define the question: Consult the 21-service directory and, where appropriate, compare a standard consultation with a VIP proposal. A higher fee does not by itself imply better fit.
  2. Review the actual scope: Each service page now provides a hypothetical worked example, practical preparation, professional limits and a comparison with a narrower or related service. Examples are not testimonials or promised results.
  3. Enquire before commissioning when necessary: The initial enquiry page supports a reviewed email draft and, where available, consented interest through an email-verified account. You must actually send an email draft; it does not submit itself.
  4. Agree on the work: For extended or high-value programmes, settle the number and duration of meetings, revision allowance, document handling, expected deliverable, deadlines and cancellation conditions in writing before paying.
  5. Pay through a verified route: Only the active first-party checkout may initiate a payment. A browser return, a screenshot or a selected package does not certify settlement. International payment options must be confirmed separately.
  6. Keep the record and arrange delivery: Retain a verified reference and confirmation email. A proposed appointment becomes confirmed only after capacity and scheduling are accepted; manuscript or report delivery follows the separately agreed scope.

Do not include identity documents, identifiable child records, unpublished confidential manuscripts or full card credentials in an open initial enquiry. Review the customer terms and privacy policy before payment.

After an order is confirmed

Keep the confirmation email and order reference for follow-up. For a printed book, verify that the recipient and postal details supplied at checkout are accurate. For an academic service, review the agreed scope and use email to arrange any next step. If the confirmation is missing or the payment status is unclear, contact support with the reference rather than creating a duplicate payment attempt.

Payment problems

If money was debited but the order status is uncertain, do not pay again. Retain the transaction reference and email the official contact address. Never send card passwords, CVV or complete card details.
