Purchase and order tracking guide

Before you pay

Review the book or service description, stated price, scope and exclusions. Checkout is available only when the relevant payment category is enabled. A return from a bank gateway is not by itself proof of successful payment; the transaction must be verified by the payment system.

Printed books

Physical orders require recipient and delivery details, including address and postal code. The seller covers shipping charges under the stated book-delivery policy. Delivery timing depends on confirmed stock and destination.

Academic and VIP services

Service fees cover the stated professional scope and do not guarantee examination results, admission or employment. Session scheduling and any agreed deliverables are coordinated by email after order confirmation.

Payment problems

If money was debited but the order status is uncertain, do not pay again. Retain the transaction reference and email the official contact address. Never send card passwords, CVV or complete card details.
