Where knowledge meets care

A book can help a talent flourish

Here, books are offered for more than reading. Each one can become a way of opening a door to the future for a promising student whose opportunities have been limited not by ability, but by financial circumstances.

We believe that gifted and talented students in Iran should not be prevented from learning and developing because their families cannot afford essential educational resources. The purpose of this bookstore is therefore cultural and educational before it is commercial: to help make books, learning opportunities and other essential forms of support accessible to talented students facing financial hardship.

Book sales provide an opportunity for readers to participate in that effort. There is particular meaning in reading a book while knowing that the same purchase may also help another person continue their education.

May the privilege of taking part in this shared endeavour belong to everyone who contributes through a purchase, an offered publication or another form of support.

In this bookstore, a book is not only an end in itself; it is also a bridge between the knowledge we share today and the opportunities we help create tomorrow.

An invitation to contribute

Authors and publishers: share your work in support of talented students

This bookstore is not limited to our publications. We welcome authors, researchers, translators and publishers who wish to make their work available for the benefit of talented students who have limited financial resources.

If you hold the necessary rights to distribute and sell a digital edition, or have valid authorisation from the rights holder, you may email us a PDF of your book, its bibliographic details and your consent to participate. After reviewing publication rights and agreeing in writing on the terms, the book may be offered here as a digital edition, with the allocable proceeds directed towards educational support for students in need.

Transparency and publishing rights: Emailing a file does not itself grant permission to publish it. Distribution requires confirmed rights and express consent. Before any participating title is sold, its price, unavoidable payment-processing expenses or applicable statutory deductions, calculation of allocable proceeds and reporting approach will be agreed in writing. The programme intends to allocate eligible proceeds to student support; it does not claim that a donation has already been made before a verified disbursement. Student identities and private information remain protected. Never send passwords or sensitive payment credentials by email.
