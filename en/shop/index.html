Where knowledge meets care

A book can help a talent flourish

Here, books are offered for more than reading. Each one can become a way of opening a door to the future for a promising student whose opportunities have been limited not by ability, but by financial circumstances.

We believe that gifted and talented students in Iran should not be prevented from learning and developing because their families cannot afford essential educational resources. The purpose of this bookstore is therefore cultural and educational before it is commercial: to help make books, learning opportunities and other essential forms of support accessible to talented students facing financial hardship.

Book sales provide an opportunity for readers to participate in that effort. There is particular meaning in reading a book while knowing that the same purchase may also help another person continue their education.

May the privilege of taking part in this shared endeavour belong to everyone who contributes through a purchase, an offered publication or another form of support.

In this bookstore, a book is not only an end in itself; it is also a bridge between the knowledge we share today and the opportunities we help create tomorrow.

Educational opportunity and talent development

Potential cannot be understood independently of opportunity

The educational perspective developed in «کشف مسیر طلایی استعداد» recognises that observed achievement is shaped in part by access to learning materials, opportunities for practice, guidance and supportive environments. Limited resources should therefore not be interpreted as a stable lack of ability or motivation. Supporting talented students who face financial constraints is, in this sense, also an attempt to make educational opportunity more equitable and to create conditions in which previously unseen strengths may become visible.

A responsible book-support initiative should be clear about its purpose and the practical arrangements for receiving, allocating and reporting contributions, while protecting the privacy and dignity of the students concerned. Educational assistance should expand agency and opportunity without turning recipients into public examples or attaching fixed labels to their circumstances.

16useful answers

01Buying and delivery

How can I order a book?

Choose a title in the catalogue, review its confirmed price and use the cart to submit delivery details. Order capture does not mean payment is complete: the bank result must be verified by the server.

Is shipping charged separately?

The seller covers shipping for printed books under the current policy. Availability and delivery details are confirmed for each order.

When will my book arrive?

Delivery depends on confirmed stock and destination. No fixed date is guaranteed before review. Follow up by email with your order reference.

What details are needed for printed books?

A recipient name, valid delivery address and postal code are needed. Formal account verification and communications are email-based; phone verification is not used.

Does every book have a digital edition?

No. Formats are offered only when verified for each title. Digital distribution also depends on confirmed publishing rights and permission.

What if my account is debited but the order is unconfirmed?

Do not pay again. Keep the order reference, amount and transaction date, and email official support. Never send card passwords, CVV codes or complete card numbers.

How do I obtain a payment receipt?

A valid receipt is available only after the website confirms the bank's payment result. A self-completed printable form is not an official bank receipt or tax invoice; consult the receipt guide.

What if a printed book arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact support with the order reference and non-sensitive evidence. Returns, replacements and refunds are assessed under applicable consumer protection rules.

02Student support and collaboration

Is buying a book the same as contributing directly?

No. A purchase is a separate commercial order. Voluntary contributions have distinct records; allocable proceeds from book sales require calculation and documented allocation.

May I contribute without buying a book?

Yes. Guest support payments do not require a book purchase or account, provided the gateway reports that it is ready.

Which contribution amounts are accepted?

Enter any whole-toman amount from 1,000 to 10,000,000. The six suggested amounts—from 200,000 to 10 million toman—are optional.

Can I fund tuition, books or clothing?

You may select a support area, but that choice does not designate a specific student. Actual allocation requires verified needs and expenditure records.

Can a sponsor and student see each other's identity?

Not automatically. Limited disclosure requires valid consent, appropriate agreement from the student and guardian, and child-safeguarding approval. Consent may be withdrawn.

May a sponsor communicate directly with a student?

No. Direct messaging, calls and exchange of private contact or address details are not supported. Access is limited to approved records.

Are these tax-deductible registered-charity donations?

No such charitable registration or tax-exempt status is claimed. Contributions use the site's merchant account and verified allocations require supporting records.

How can authors and publishers collaborate?

Email the work details and evidence of distribution rights. Sale of digital copies and allocation of proceeds require rights review and a written agreement.
