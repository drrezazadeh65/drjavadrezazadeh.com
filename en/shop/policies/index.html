OFFICIAL COMMERCE POLICY · 4.4

Purchases, delivery and order support

Before paying, check the item, quantity, amount, customer details and delivery address. Creating an order or returning from the gateway does not prove payment; a server-verified transaction does.

1. Printed books

The three published poetry collections, Sepid, Tariki and Bonbast, have an owner-approved listed price of 2,000,000 toman per title. The server determines final pricing, and sales depend on confirmed catalogue availability.

2. Delivery

Shipping of physical books is free to the buyer, with the delivery cost borne by the store. A recipient name, phone, province, city, full delivery address and valid postal code are required. A shipment tracking reference is displayed once a fulfilment operator records it. Actual delivery dates depend on stock, preparation and the delivery carrier; no unverified delivery date is guaranteed.

3. Professional services and VIP packages

Each service's defined scope, exclusions and published price apply. Scheduling and capacity must be coordinated following review of the request. Educational, publication and professional outcomes are not guaranteed, and additional work requires a separate agreement.

4. Payment and receipt

Orders have unique identifiers. A payment is recognised only after gateway-side server verification. A verified payment receipt can be viewed or printed using its private access link. Such a receipt is not necessarily a legally compliant tax invoice. When a debit is uncertain, do not pay again until support confirms the status.

5. Cancellation and refunds

To request cancellation, address delivery discrepancies or damage, or change a service booking, contact official support with the order number. Requests are considered according to fulfilment status, agreed service conditions and applicable law. A refund is only regarded as complete after the actual return of funds is independently recorded and verified.

6. Customer privacy

Customer identity, optional email and delivery details are collected for order fulfilment and support. They are not shared on public pages or transmitted to marketing analytics. Detailed receipts require a private capability or authorised administrative access.
