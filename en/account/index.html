How scheduling works

You choose the format; the consultant confirms a time after reviewing internal capacity.

Standard consultations offer telephone or online video. In-person sessions are limited to explicitly catalogued VIP or annual coaching services in Tehran or Kashan.

Choosing a format is not a confirmed booking. A booking does not prove bank settlement or professional service delivery. Rescheduling and cancellation are not yet available here.

Trust for high-value services comes from operational clarity.

A serious purchase is defensible only when the client can see what is being bought, what outcome is expected, how payment becomes authoritative, where private records live, and how delivery continues afterwards. The dashboard keeps that chain in one accountable workspace.
