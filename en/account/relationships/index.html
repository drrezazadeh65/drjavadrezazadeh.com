Private Access Preview

Relationships

A role does not create access until the relationship is verified.

Production access is relationship-scoped. A parent sees only authorised child relationships; a teacher sees only assigned students and the data categories allowed by the assignment scope.

Parent relationships

No verified relationship in this preview.

Possible states are Requested, Pending verification, Active, Rejected, Revoked and Expired. Only Active relationships can unlock the authorised parent view.

Teacher assignments

No teacher assignment in this preview.

Teacher access is assignment-based and may be Active, Suspended or Ended. Assignment scope controls which student records are visible.

Access rule

Role + verified relationship + record scope.

Knowing a student, sharing an email address, or selecting “parent” or “teacher” during registration is never sufficient to access student evidence.
