Service boundary

Not every question should become a paid service.

Public explanations, methodology pages and academic resources remain available without forcing users into consultation. Paid or private services should begin only where individual evidence, professional review, private records or scheduled support become necessary.

Private platform

When a service needs personal data, it moves into the secure account layer.

Student records, RCAS responses, private reports, appointments, messages and payments are designed to remain outside the public web and will become active only after production authentication and backend controls are connected.
