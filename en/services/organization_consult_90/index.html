Professional rationale

For a school or organisation needing to define an educational problem and an initial course of action. Effective professional engagement begins with a precise problem formulation and the careful identification of evidence rather than an assumption that any general package can resolve every situation.

Institutional problems require explicit attention to stakeholders, constraints and intended measures of progress. Before promising a programme intervention, the consultation should distinguish the presenting concern from the underlying organizational conditions and agree what evidence could justify a next step.
