Academic evidence and interpretive limitations

A transcript records performance within a particular curriculum and marking system. Sound interpretation therefore considers the design and limits of the assessment before making claims about a student's longer-term capability or suitability; uncertainty belongs in the analysis, not only in its small print.

Before you request this service

You have a score report or transcript but need to understand what it does and does not indicate for an educational decision.

What to bring

Identify the type and date of assessment, the decision it is meant to inform and relevant context. Sensitive score reports must only be exchanged through an agreed secure channel.

What the work can establish

The assessment context, possible sources of score variation and reasonable inferences are examined. A score cannot establish a fixed talent profile or predict future success by itself.

Illustrative scenario, not a client outcome

Illustrative case: lower mathematics scores on two different tests do not necessarily show declining ability; item difficulty, test conditions and patterns of error also matter.
