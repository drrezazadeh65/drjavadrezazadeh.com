Problem and professional rationale

For educators with a specific challenge in teaching, learning materials or assessment. The relevant question is what evidence can responsibly be used to inform the decision. The service is framed as a bounded, transparent engagement rather than a deterministic solution to every case.

Teaching difficulties rarely have a context-free solution: the learning goals, learners, classroom resources and assessment evidence interact. The consultation therefore examines the specific pedagogical question before recommending any change in materials or classroom practice.

Expected work and limits

A 60-minute problem analysis with viable professional options. The practical value of the service rests on a clear account of what has been considered, which assumptions remain tentative and what can reasonably be acted upon.

Not included: Full course design or a whole-institution project is outside this session.

How the service is conducted

  1. Identify the defined problem and the professional scope.
  2. Clarify the sources of information and the limits of their interpretation.
  3. Review the material and prepare the agreed analysis or output.
  4. Communicate the outcome and any appropriate next step.
