Academic evidence and limits of interpretation

A research CV should make real contributions in teaching, scholarship and service legible to the relevant selection committee. Responsible research assessment cautions against reducing individual quality to journal-level metrics, and professional presentation must never inflate authorship, appointments or achievements.
