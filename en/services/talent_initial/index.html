Problem and professional rationale

For beginning a multi-source review of strengths and development potential before major decisions. The relevant question is what evidence can responsibly be used to inform the decision. The service is framed as a bounded, transparent engagement rather than a deterministic solution to every case.

An initial assessment should open a question about growth rather than close a student's opportunities. A single questionnaire or observation cannot establish a stable lifelong talent category; different sources and environments must be considered before any recommendation is made.

Expected work and limits

An initial evidence profile and identification of questions needing further examination. The practical value of the service rests on a clear account of what has been considered, which assumptions remain tentative and what can reasonably be acted upon.

Not included: No definitive talent label, IQ designation or clinical diagnosis is produced.
