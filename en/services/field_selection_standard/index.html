The professional question

For applicants who need systematic, defensible priorities among programme and university choices. A responsible engagement begins by establishing which decision requires support and which information can legitimately inform it. A service label alone cannot replace this clarification.

An ordered preference list should express the applicant's priorities while remaining consistent with verified eligibility, institutional requirements and official admission information. Guidance is therefore a process of transparent comparison, not a promise that a particular programme will offer admission.

What this service delivers

A structured development and review of preferences grounded in the applicant’s verified information. The deliverable is framed in terms of evidence, traceable reasoning and a practicable next step rather than a guaranteed personal or institutional outcome.

Scope and exclusions

Academic evidence and interpretive limitations

A defensible programme shortlist distinguishes verified admission rules from unofficial predictions and makes the applicant’s own priorities explicit. Labour-market information can broaden the comparison, but access to information alone does not establish which educational path will serve one individual best.

Before you request this service

An applicant in the Iranian admission system wants a reasoned ranking of university programmes consistent with official rules and personal preferences.

What to bring

Bring the applicable official admission handbook, verified scores or eligibility data, preferred disciplines and geographic and financial constraints.

What the work can establish

Available options are compared against the current official rules, the applicant's priorities and any uncertainty in the evidence. The applicant remains responsible for the final official submission.

Illustrative scenario, not a client outcome

Illustrative case: two programmes look equally attractive academically but entail different living costs. A comparison table can clarify why an applicant prefers one.
