Professional rationale

For authors responding to up to 20 reviewer comments and seeking a systematic scholarly response. Effective professional engagement begins with a precise problem formulation and the careful identification of evidence rather than an assumption that any general package can resolve every situation.

A productive response to peer review separates each comment, the author's reasoned reply and the exact change made to the manuscript. When the author respectfully disagrees, the explanation should remain evidence-based and transparent; the service cannot assert findings or changes that have not been made.

Academic evidence and limits of interpretation

A response to peer review should allow each comment to be traced to an actual textual revision or an explicit, evidence-based reason for disagreement. Transparency is consistent with scientific judgement: it does not require uncritical acceptance of every suggestion, but excludes fabricated analyses and concealed departures from the evidence.
