Can I buy an examination now?

Purchasing and registration remain unavailable until the question set, answer key, timekeeping, payment and access control have passed acceptance.

Does a score predict admission?

No. Descriptive performance may inform further study, but prediction requires separate external validation and authentic outcomes.
