Why this matters

Academic engagement is not decoration around research.

Presenting a paper, reviewing the work of others, organising a scholarly event or returning to a workshop as a learner all create forms of answerability that solitary writing cannot provide. They expose assumptions, sharpen questions and place professional judgement in relation to a wider community. For that reason, I treat academic engagement as part of scholarly practice rather than a separate list of credentials.
