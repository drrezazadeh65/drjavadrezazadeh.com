Private consultation request

Request consultation or professional service

Start with the problem, purpose and expected form of help—not with a large file transfer.

This route supports student guidance, academic services, teacher support and institutional enquiries while asking only for the minimum information needed at the first step. Sensitive educational records and confidential manuscripts should not be sent until a suitable exchange method is agreed.

How can you make a real service enquiry?

Two routes are offered. Preparing an email draft is not the same as sending it, and an initial enquiry is not a confirmed appointment or payment.

Without an account: send the email yourself

Use the email enquiry composer to describe your question and choose the kind of help you need. Open the draft in your email application and actually press Send. If your mail application does not open, copy the draft and send it to the published contact address.

Verified account: consented initial interest

Where account access and the consented-intake service are available, use the authenticated interest form. This submits only minimal service-selection information, not a private manuscript, student report or a guaranteed booking.

Do not submit passports, phone numbers, examination records, identifiable child information or confidential unpublished manuscripts in an initial open message. Exact scope, availability, time zone, fees and delivery arrangements require a separate agreement; overseas payment cannot be assumed to work.

Boundary

Consultation does not guarantee an outcome.

Advice is bounded by the information available, the educational context and the purpose of the consultation. Admission, examination, employment and future-success outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
