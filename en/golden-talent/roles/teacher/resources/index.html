Teacher Observation Resources

Contribute educational evidence without converting classroom impressions into fixed labels.

These resources define how role-based evidence should be contributed and interpreted once secure accounts are active.

Describe observable learning behavior

Record what the learner did, produced or changed—not a personality judgement.

Compare within context

Difficulty in one task or teaching context does not automatically generalize to a broad ability claim.

Use error productively

Errors can show strategy, misconception, persistence and responsiveness to feedback.

Notice opportunity

Ask whether the learner has actually had access to instruction, practice and relevant tasks.

Flag uncertainty

A strong observation includes what the teacher does not yet know.

Boundary

Good evidence is concrete, scoped and revisable.

The platform does not ask parents or teachers to diagnose the student or decide a fixed future. Their role is to add context-rich evidence that can be considered alongside other sources.
