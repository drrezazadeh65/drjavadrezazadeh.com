Six complementary evidence lenses

Evidence matters more than a permanent label.

01

Interest evidence

Which first-hand experiences sustain this interest beyond an initial impression?

02

Competence evidence

Which observable performance samples support or question the hypothesis?

03

Experience evidence

Has this direction been explored through a course, task, project or comparable activity?

04

Feedback evidence

What specific feedback has been received from appropriately knowledgeable people?

05

World evidence

What credible information about study, work and access constraints is available?

06

Uncertainty

What remains unknown, inconsistent, contextual or dependent on further observation?
