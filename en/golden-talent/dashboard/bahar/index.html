Build your learning and action plan

Describe one goal, an observable action and practical evidence. This is a working educational worksheet, not a scored assessment. Your responses stay in this page's memory only and disappear when you reload or leave; nothing is uploaded. Designed for adult self-directed use until separately approved child safeguarding is available.

Seven-day descriptive learning log

For each day, describe an action, what actually happened, and an uncertainty or rival explanation. A blank day is not failure or lack of ability. Nothing is saved or transferred; print a review or personally store it securely to continue on another day.

Formative action cycle · guided review

From context to revisable action

The following sequence represents the educational action cycle already used in the Persian workspace. It is not a validated developmental diagnostic model.

Recognise context and direction↓Choose a strategy and action↓Act on the plan↓Monitor and collect evidence↓Interpret gaps and errors↓Revise the strategy or goal↓Return to action

Six planned continuity components · not yet active

From a baseline to reviewed action

These are six planned portfolio components, distinct from the seven-phase formative action cycle above. They correspond to the Persian workspace without implying an activated learner record or scientifically validated growth outcome.

01

Baseline snapshot

The learner's starting context, permitted evidence and unresolved uncertainties; not a diagnostic profile.

02

Eight-week compass

One chosen goal, a reason it matters, observable evidence of progress and relevant constraints.

03

Weekly BAHAR cycle

Goal, action, observed evidence, interpretation, revision and next review.

04

Learning evidence log

Performance samples, recurring errors, feedback and contexts with their respective provenance.

05

Four-week review

Continue, change, pause or investigate according to the available evidence and professional judgement.

06

BAHAR 30-day starter

Week 1: baseline. Week 2: action and context. Week 3: feedback and error patterns. Week 4: review and revision.

Boundary

BAHAR supports action; it does not replace professional judgement.

Production use will preserve review history and distinguish student reflection from adviser interpretation.
