D5 · Golden Path

Which educational pathways currently deserve further exploration, and what evidence would strengthen or challenge them?

This page exposes the scientific structure and evidence task only. Real responses, scoring, interpretation and longitudinal storage remain locked until authenticated backend and entitlement verification are active.

Interest evidence

Look for sustained engagement, not momentary preference.

Competence evidence

Use actual performance and learning evidence.

Experience evidence

Ask whether the learner has tried enough real experiences to judge the path.

Valid feedback

Include feedback from people who have observed relevant performance.

World reality

Consider entry requirements, study demands, work reality and access.

Adaptability

Keep the path revisable as evidence changes.
