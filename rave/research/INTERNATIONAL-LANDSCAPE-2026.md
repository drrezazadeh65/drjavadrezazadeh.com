# RAVE international landscape and novelty assessment — 2026-10-09

## Research conclusion
The constituent capabilities of autonomous publishing, social listening, AI visibility tracking, CRM workflows and marketing automation are not unprecedented. The research hypothesis is a portable, website-native, academic-identity-aware growth operating system that connects verified scholarly claims, multilingual content, ethical distribution, earned media, conversion and auditability.

## Benchmarks
- Buffer: multi-network publishing, channel-specific formatting, scheduling and analytics. https://support.buffer.com/articles/supported-channels-LM3P7Y4zsp
- Hootsuite: social channel/account support and management. https://help.hootsuite.com/s/article/connect-social
- Sprout Social: social listening, publishing, reporting, workflow intelligence and AI agents. https://sproutsocial.com/features/
- HubSpot: CRM, marketing automation, lead workflows and social triggers. https://knowledge.hubspot.com/get-started/automate-your-processes
- Semrush: AI visibility measurement, prompt tracking and SEO. https://www.semrush.com/solutions/ai-visibility/
- n8n: reusable automation workflows, including social distribution. https://n8n.io/workflows/categories/social-media/

## Architectural consequences
1. Do not claim first-in-world novelty. Investigate defensible differentiation through implementation and empirical evaluation.
2. Use a channel capability registry instead of hardcoded assumptions about posting APIs.
3. Separate discovery, publishing, listening, scholarly profiles, search indexing, messaging and commerce: these are different capabilities.
4. API access, scopes, rates, approval, media requirements and geographic availability are per-adapter release gates.
5. Do not manufacture backlinks or automate manipulative link schemes. Google policy: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
6. Google Indexing API is not a general-purpose article indexing API: https://developers.google.com/search/apis/indexing-api/v3/quickstart
7. No promise of AI inclusion or ranking; sample visibility and citation evidence.
8. Minors, research ethics, email opt-in, anti-spam and credential safety are mandatory controls.
9. Run GitHub-hosted source and portable Node core, with optional approved API adapters; GitHub Pages remains replaceable.
10. Confirm all platform permissions and terms again immediately before implementing live publication.

## Validation plan
Competitor feature matrix -> channel API feasibility matrix -> 45-article content inventory -> consent and rights audit -> prototype on two approved channels -> attribution study -> expand adapters by evidence, not channel count.
