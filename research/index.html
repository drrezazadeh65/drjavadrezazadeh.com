Research

Human-centred educational research

The research programme connects empirical work in language education and assessment with a broader concern: how educational systems can remain technically defensible while responding to the human beings affected by them.

Established research base

The programme grows from work in applied linguistics, English-language education, learner proficiency and fluency, language assessment, curriculum and materials evaluation, mixed-methods inquiry and technology-enhanced learning. The doctoral research examined cultural and pedagogical fit in EFL materials, while earlier work investigated relationships among lexical fluency, temporal fluency and language proficiency.

Humanability

Humanability is being developed as a research programme concerned with the human dimension of assessment. It asks what may be missed when assessment quality is discussed only through conventional technical criteria, and how consequences, experience, context and professional judgement can be examined without weakening requirements for validity, reliability and fairness.

TESTLY

TESTLY is an emerging integrative framework for examining testing quality through multiple related technical and human dimensions. It is being developed as a wider research and book programme rather than presented here as a completed or validated measurement model.

Teacher Humanization

Teacher Humanization examines formative relationships, professional judgement and the ways educational actors encounter the consequences of their formative power. The project is under theoretical development and is being subjected to comparison with adjacent traditions rather than being treated as established theory by assertion.

Golden Talent

The talent-development programme is designed around multi-source evidence rather than one-test labelling. Its longer-term infrastructure is being built to distinguish student self-report, parent information, teacher observation, academic records and assessment-derived evidence, while preserving instrument versions and research provenance for later validation studies.
