The sum is USD 10,000, but every price, direct cost, refund allowance, delivery hour and win rate is **hypothetical**, not a proposed rate card, actual booking or market observation. Required time must include preparation, delivery, revision, follow-up and overhead; do not publish capacity outside genuine administrator-controlled hours.
