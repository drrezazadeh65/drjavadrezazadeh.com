### Scientific interpretation constraint

The book proposal explicitly describes TESTLY as a **provisional integrative meta-framework**, not a replacement for measurement science or a validated scalar index. Fairability is expressly a **working term** whose added scientific value remains an open question. The Humanability proposal calls for discriminant validity against fairness, wellbeing and experience, allowing the possibility that the distinct construct may have to be revised or rejected. No automatic weights, norms, item-response parameters, scientifically validated thresholds, aggregate TESTLY score or certification can be invented.
