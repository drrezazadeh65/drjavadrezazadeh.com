> **Interpretive principle:** Scholarly excellence cannot be reduced to obtaining an indexing badge. A journal earns academic standing through original research, accountable and independent peer review, demonstrable integrity, sustained contributions to its field, stable long-term accessibility and meaningful engagement across scholarly communities. DOAJ, Scopus, ESCI, SSCI and a first-quartile position are separately awarded or calculated outcomes, not products a publisher can promise.

This annex operationalises the user's request for a long-range pathway towards international scholarly prominence and aligns it with the existing master design, legal and technical safeguards. The 10 public phases are stored in `platform/jhela-international-roadmap-v1.json`. Future dates are **conditional on evidence**, not contractual promises. Parallel work is encouraged where it does not bypass prerequisites.

## B. Working programmes behind the ten phases

### 1. Scholarly identity, scope and responsible internationalisation
JHELA's distinct intellectual contribution should not rest on a broad promise to publish "all education". Its human-centred orientation should connect learning, assessment, language education, professional judgement, curriculum, educational technology and student development through defensible methodological and interpretive scholarship. Research situated in particular countries or educational systems remains welcome when its conceptual contribution, evidence and limits of transferability are explained. Verify the journal title against existing serials; publish lawful publisher identity, real mailing address, ownership and independent governance before inviting formal articles.

### 2. Native editorial infrastructure rather than simulated dashboards
Retain the existing public website and bilingual discovery pages. Deploy supported PKP OJS only on the independently verified intended host `journal.drjavadrezazadeh.com`, with dedicated private DB credentials, non-web-accessible manuscript files, TLS signed by a public CA, verified effective PHP version/extensions, actual SMTP receipt, password recovery, role-based authorization, secure session handling and independently tested backup restoration. Avoid unauthorised custom OJS forks or a parallel PHP manuscript system; use documented native extension points and configurable review forms. The public author and journal workstream dashboards remain informational until OJS can enforce their intended permissions. Previously observed self-signed TLS and nested `/journals/` access concern must be closed with dated evidence, not a source flag.

### 3. Editorial independence and ethical quality control
Require explicit, informed board appointment consent; verify affiliations, subject expertise and appropriate method competence. For ordinary research articles, two genuinely distinct independent external reviewers are the default; distinguish confidential handling-editor notes from author-facing review. Declarations cover competing interests, authorship, participant safeguards (especially children), ethics approvals where applicable, funding, data/material availability, corrections, retractions, AI assistance and confidentiality. Manuscripts authored by the founding editor or associates must be delegated to an unconflicted independent handling editor. Reviewer ratings are diagnostic; numerical sums cannot make acceptance decisions.

**DOAJ quality controls, independently verified:** it requires at least five research articles per year, a credible editorial board (at least five qualified editors recommended), and the share of published research with a board/editor/reviewer author must not exceed 25% in either of the last two issues (or for continuous publication, in the relevant previous calendar year at a minimum five-article output). Monitor these limits without excluding meritorious independent research on national grounds. <https://doaj.org/apply/guide/>

### 4. Sustainable research publication before index application
Launch only after OJS works end-to-end. Select a publication-frequency policy the journal can genuinely maintain, rather than manufacturing a large inaugural issue to simulate scale. The founding issue remains covered by the **100% APC waiver** already declared. Record author submission, acceptance and publication dates, anonymised reviewer handling, stable issue/article URLs, proof signoff, accessible PDF or HTML, open licence and copyright display. Maintain adequate real publishing history before index review. The journal must not publicise fabricated acceptance rates, time-to-first-decision metrics or indexing successes.

### 5. Academic identifiers, metadata and preservation
Obtain an ISSN through the competent national ISSN agency and verify the journal's registered title; never self-generate an ISSN. If legally eligible, seek Crossref membership, including sponsorship where appropriate, and ensure **all articles going forward** receive accurate and persistent DOI records as required by Crossref terms. Metadata should include verified authors/affiliations, ORCIDs where present, full references, funding acknowledgements and correction relationships. A DOI must resolve to a unique, enduring landing page; an unregistered identifier in a PDF is not a DOI. Preserve authentic content through approved preservation services, versioned backups and tested independent restore procedures.

**Cost planning reference — not an activated payment:** Crossref's 2026 fee schedule lists base annual membership tiers beginning at **USD 200** for publishing revenue/expenses below USD 1,000 and **USD 275** for the next tier, plus a **USD 1.00** 2026 registration fee per current journal-article record. These are provider tariffs, not JHELA author charges, and must be reconfirmed at the application date; Crossref can impose country/legal restrictions and fees may change. <https://www.crossref.org/fees/>; <https://www.crossref.org/membership/>.

### 6. DOAJ pathway
The first gate is actual, regularly published research (DOAJ minimum **five research articles annually**). A newly launched journal must demonstrate **more than one year of publishing history OR at least ten open-access research articles**, then also satisfy all other eligibility, rights, licensing, publisher-contact, editorial, peer-review and transparency requirements. These are not a guarantee of acceptance. DOAJ listing must not be advertised while an application is pending or has been refused. <https://doaj.org/apply/guide/>.

### 7. Scopus pathway
Scopus requires peer-reviewed content and an explanation of review, regular publication, registered ISSN, publication history, English-language abstracts/titles and publicly available publication ethics. The expert Content Selection and Advisory Board evaluates policy, scientific contribution, editorial/author geography, academic standing, publication regularity and online availability. The old two-year waiting requirement for new journals was removed in **August 2024**, but there is still a requirement for sufficient published content and a meaningful evaluable record. Acceptance and possible subject-ranking quartiles remain independent external decisions. <https://www.elsevier.com/products/scopus/content/content-policy-and-selection>; <https://blog.scopus.com/scopus-content-policy-and-selection-changes/>.

### 8. Web of Science, ESCI and later SSCI
Clarivate currently evaluates journals against **24 quality and four impact criteria**. New candidate journals passing the quality evaluation are admitted to ESCI; further selection to SSCI, SCIE or AHCI is impact-dependent and controlled independently. ESCI is not the same as SSCI, and neither an ESCI label nor a Journal Impact Factor automatically establishes Q1. Quartiles are relative to annual category comparisons: any future claim must state database, metric, subject category and year with verified source evidence. Only the publisher may submit new journal evaluations through Clarivate's Publisher Portal where eligible. <https://clarivate.com/academia-government/scientific-and-academic-research/research-discovery-and-referencing/web-of-science/web-of-science-core-collection/editorial-selection-process/journal-evaluation-process-selection-criteria/>.

### 9. Global discoverability and research partnerships
Develop relevant international editorial participation and evidence-based scholarly exchanges, not purchased affiliations. Maintain high-quality article abstracts and metadata, research ethics literacy, accessible full text and relevant disciplinary outreach. Establish legitimate relationships with associations, teacher educators and researchers while separating scholarly programmes from consultancy and paid publishing services. Citation growth must occur through independent research uptake, not reciprocal coercive citations, purchased metrics or citation-cartel behaviour.

### 10. Ethical financial sustainability
The publicly declared **prospective** APC schedule is CHF 0 on submission/rejection; CHF 2,000 for full research, systematic review, methods and theory papers; CHF 990 for short research reports and perspectives; free corrections/retractions; and a complete first-issue waiver. No income should be estimated as bank-verified until independently reconciled transactions exist. The prices are **not due today** and should not be represented as equivalent quality or ranking to an established publisher simply because their nominal fees are similar. Business models should consider sponsorship, institutional partnerships and grants but preserve absolute editorial autonomy. Verify the lawful publisher/merchant, invoicing currency, refunds, tax obligations, waivers and payment platform before activating any collection.

### 11. Public communication and journal SEO
Preserve the existing independent public journal sitemap, canonical URLs and Persian/English hreflang links; include only genuine indexable information pages until real accepted articles are published. The OJS subdomain should get its own truthful sitemap after TLS, permissions and release prove it exists. Map legacy paths with one-hop 301 redirects only after verifying exact destinations. Never add fake issues, DOI or ScholarlyArticle schema. Test Google Scholar discovery through real article pages, accessible full text, quality metadata and crawler evidence; generic promotional visibility does not imply academic indexing.

### 12. Continuous assurance and culture of responsible publishing
Conduct annual independent audits of editorial conflict, article selection, fairness, endogeny, author diversity, timeliness, process transparency, data protection, correction responsiveness and long-term access. Prefer contextual interpretation of quantitative indicators to ranking chases. A prestigious journal should contribute to theory and practice while remaining accountable to the learners, teachers and institutions affected by educational research.

## C. Milestone release policy, not invented target dates

**Foundation:** JH-01–03 may be advanced concurrently but must all be verified before public manuscript intake. **Publication:** JH-04 and JH-05 are maintained through genuine publication and identifier operations. **Eligibility:** JH-06–09 are individually reviewed once credible and sufficient publication history exists; submissions and outcomes are not automatically sequential. **Long-term influence:** JH-10 depends on years of genuine scholarly engagement and externally assessed citation standing. Administrative due dates may be set after responsible staffing, capacity and infrastructure are established; do not publish external-index acceptance dates as commitments.

Every gate should have: `owner`, `source_version`, `evidence_date`, `evidence_type`, `verified_scope`, `valid_until`, `independent_reviewer`, `outcome`, `remaining_defect`. Missing evidence is **unknown/pending**, not zero progress or a passing check. Declarations of acceptance/impact must point to authoritative external records and should be reviewed when indexing decisions or rankings change.
