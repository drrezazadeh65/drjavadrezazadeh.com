## Campaign EN-01 | The problem with "This study proves..."
Audience: Researchers and doctoral candidates; LinkedIn / academic communities.
Copy:
A manuscript can be grammatically polished and still make claims that its evidence cannot support. A small observational study, for instance, may illuminate a situated educational practice without establishing a universal causal effect.

This distinction matters because academic editing and manuscript diagnosis address different tasks. Editing should preserve the writer's scholarly voice and clarify expression. Diagnostic review examines whether the argument, method, evidence and interpretation belong together.

I have added a transparent, hypothetical example of how a diagnosis might identify and prioritize revision needs—without presenting it as a client result or promising publication.
