# Golden Talent · independent multi-source judgement study pack
**Revision:** 2026-10-16-GT1 · **Status:** PREREGISTRATION CANDIDATE ONLY, NO HUMAN DATA / NO INDEPENDENT SIGNATURE.

## Target scientific claim
The Golden Talent method may assist qualified educators in forming **traceable, cautious, revisable hypotheses** from multiple educational evidence sources. The method does not authorize a unitary trait score, deterministic talent category, automated career assignment or diagnosis.

## Primary research question and rivals
Compared with standard unstructured case discussion, does the defined Golden Talent workflow increase accuracy of **source attribution**, correct identification of contradictory/absent evidence, calibrated uncertainty, justification of next action, and safeguarding of the learner's agency? Rival hypotheses: more elaborate paperwork only; motivated reasoning induced by tool labels; evaluator authority bias; and improved outcomes merely caused by additional time/explanation.

## Construct/rubric anchors
Evidence families:
- **S** — learner/parent/teacher self-report with role/provenance.
- **P** — consented performance products and authentic tasks.
- **R** — historical records (interpret with context, not as fixed ability).
- **O** — specific observed learning behaviours and their observer/source.
- **C** — contextual opportunities, barriers, accommodations and supports.
- **T** — ethically collected behaviour traces, only with approved purpose and retention.

Rate the **quality of the case analysis**, not the learner:
1. **Attribution:** statements clearly tied to valid sources, with unknowns disclosed.
2. **Discordance:** conflicting sources preserved rather than averaged away.
3. **Context:** attention to access, opportunity, socioeconomic, linguistic and disability context without stereotyping.
4. **Uncertainty calibration:** distinguishes observation, interpretation, hypothesis and recommendation.
5. **Reversibility:** proportionate, testable, low-stakes next steps; clear review trigger.
6. **Safeguarding and fairness:** age-appropriate agency, consent scope and absence of high-impact unsupported recommendations.

Scoring of **reviewer performance** (if approved): blinded rubric categories `absent, weak, acceptable, strong`; no conversion to a student talent IQ or percentile. Train raters on anchor examples independent of the actual test-set cases.

## Cases, randomisation and blinding
Develop fictional/composite **non-identifiable** vignettes with authentic educational structure. Cases deliberately vary evidence convergence, contradiction, scarcity, missingness, bias, multilingual and accessibility context. The design team must avoid copying actual pupil records into fixtures. Pre-register the rationale for case count, independent review panel qualifications, analysis method, order, randomization/crossover and handling of practice/learning effects. Require the target case answers to be independently adjudicated rather than supplied solely by the instrument author.

Separate primary and secondary outcomes; estimate uncertainty for rater agreement (e.g. weighted kappa/Gwet alternatives where appropriate), sensitivity to prevalence and uncertainty distributions, and rubric item-level disagreements. Use an appropriate precision/power or simulation justification; do not pick a magic N from the website.

## Research session checklist
1. Confirm independent, conflict-checked educational experts; role/age jurisdiction applies before real-learner work.
2. Provide identical case information to comparator and structured-workflow conditions; time exposure should be controlled or measured.
3. Record anonymized case ID, condition, order, reviewer code, stated evidence, contradictions, uncertainty language, next step and dissent.
4. Blind outcome coders to the author's preferred hypothesis, record dual ratings and resolve disagreement with a third independent adjudicator.
5. Analyze false certainty, unsupported labels and subgroup-specific disparate effects; treat adverse cases as findings, not exclusions.
6. Publish complete qualitative themes, descriptive distributions, confidence intervals if quantitative, preregistered deviations and limitations.

## Scientific acceptance gate
Before limited professional workflow claims: external methods and safeguarding panel approves the exact source definitions, response fidelity and results for a specified context. Real pupil records remain inaccessible unless the live Bertina account, guardian/assent, research-purpose consent and auditable storage permissions are separately activated. A favourable vignette comparison still **does not validate a global talent score**.

## Master visual output (non-deploying design template)
All formal reports have the approved Persian symbol, dark/ivory/gold semantic typography, bilingual version/hash line, case ID only, source map, explicit differences between **Observed / Interpreted / Proposed**, reviewer competence/conflict records, limitations, consent and revision state. No unofficial seal, validity badge, fabricated reviewer name or certification.
