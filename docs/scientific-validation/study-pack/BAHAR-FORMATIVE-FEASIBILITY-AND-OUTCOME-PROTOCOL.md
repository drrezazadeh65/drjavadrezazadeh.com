# BAHAR · staged formative feasibility and independent effectiveness study pack
**Revision:** 2026-10-16-B1 · **Status:** DESIGN ONLY; no participant data or effectiveness study.

## Current supported function
Adult-oriented bilingual browser worksheet: eight-week learning goal, a practical weekly action, five short optional observations and a *descriptive*, copyable review. Values remain in page memory; no server write, account enrollment or diagnosis. No participant record or longitudinal persistence follows from a browser test.

## Phase I — independent usability / comprehension (low-risk adult pilot, after approval)
Predefine accessibility accommodations and tasks:
1. A user explains what the worksheet can infer (no score/diagnosis).
2. The user creates a specific, feasible learning goal.
3. The user selects an action within realistic time and context constraints.
4. The user identifies observable but non-sensitive progress evidence.
5. The user revises a plan based on incomplete or conflicting evidence.
6. The user locates how to copy, erase or leave without saving.
7. The user identifies what would require professional support.

Observe completion/failure, understandability, task burden, perceived pressure or distress, keyboard/screen-reader performance, mobile layout and explicit user control. Define error severity independently and record negative cases. User competence is **not** scored; a failed interaction is a product design finding, not a learner trait.

## Phase II — observational feasibility of longitudinal BAHAR
Only after separate authenticated persistent storage, purpose-specific consent and approved retention:
- Define starting goal, baseline context, action plan, weekly cycle, evidence origin, next decision, constraints and human review.
- Feasibility metrics: opt-in/revocation rates, missing/incomplete cycles, time cost, unequal access, reviewer workload, participant burden and identifiable harms.
- Distinguish *evidence presence* from *learning progress*, and *user satisfaction* from *effectiveness*.
- If expert rubrics are added, independently appraise clarity, validity for their intended interpretation, rater calibration and agreement. No unvalidated composite BAHAR score.

## Phase III — effectiveness of a specific educational intervention
Prerequisites: precise external outcome measure selected for intended context; prospective study protocol with equipoise/ethics, comparison structure, planned measurement points, intervention fidelity and version lock, plausible mechanisms and alternative explanations. Prefer controlled or otherwise credible comparison appropriate to educational settings; avoid randomization when inappropriate or unethical and transparently address confounding.

Analysis: denominators, engagement bias, baseline differences, missingness/attrition, effect magnitude with uncertainty, subgroup fairness, alternative causal explanations, unintended costs and relevance to everyday learning. **A series of completed weekly journals alone cannot prove improvement**.

## Participant information drafts (adult only; independent approval required)
**فارسی:** «BAHAR یک کاربرگ برنامه‌ریزی و بازاندیشی آموزشی است. در نسخه کنونی، نوشته‌های شما به سرور ارسال نمی‌شوند و با خروج از صفحه از دست می‌روند. این فعالیت نمره استاندارد، تشخیص یا گواهی رشد ارائه نمی‌کند. اگر در پژوهشی درباره کارایی و تجربه استفاده شرکت کنید، اطلاع‌رسانی و رضایت مستقلی با شرح دقیق داده‌های گردآوری‌شده، نگهداری و انصراف قبل از شروع ارائه خواهد شد.»

**English:** “BAHAR is a formative learning-plan worksheet. In the current browser edition, your entries are not uploaded and are lost on leaving the page. The activity provides no standardised score, diagnosis or certified evidence of progress. Any separate research study will require a new approved information sheet explaining data collection, retention and withdrawal before participation.”

## Product and scientific no-go
No hidden client persistence, analytics capture of input, private child records, advice concerning diagnosis/therapy, automated talent ranking, verified effectiveness claims, or payment for unqualified psychometric reports.

## Reviewable outputs
Protocol/preregistration, versioned user flow, consent notices, observer guide, accessibility notes, cognitive task recordings only if approved, qualitative codebook, fidelity measures, adverse events, complete analysis and independent review. Preserve the luxury mother brand (approved Persian mark, shared golden accents) and accessible bilingual layout rather than inventing a competing BAHAR subbrand.

**Approval state:** no recruitment/ethics/empirical effect record currently verified. Proceed only with the public adult in-browser formative exercise until all live safeguarding and study gates are signed.
