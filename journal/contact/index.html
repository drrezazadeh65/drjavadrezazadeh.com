Contact

Founding editorial office

Editorial enquiries during the founding stage may be directed to dr.rezazadeh65@gmail.com. A dedicated journal address will replace this contact once the independent journal domain and mail infrastructure are established.

Scholarly and editorial enquiries

During the founding period, the editorial office welcomes professional enquiries from potential reviewers and researchers interested in independent editorial collaboration. Applications should identify the intended role in the email subject and provide a concise professional profile, research interests and verifiable institutional information where available. Formal manuscripts should not be submitted to this address until the editorial system opens.

The journal intends to establish a dedicated mailbox on its permanent subdomain once the required hosting, security and editorial service are in place. Its public contact address and the identity of the independent publisher will then be updated with verifiable information. No unverified postal address or institutional relationship will be displayed.

Which enquiry belongs here?

Prospective reviewers should consult the reviewer call and peer-review policy before introducing their scholarly background and methodological expertise. Editorial collaborators can review the founding collaboration guidance. Researchers considering a future submission should first read the journal aims and scope and author guidance; no manuscript review or acceptance can be initiated by an informal email.

Privacy and current service boundaries

Use a clear subject describing the enquiry, and include only the professional details needed to understand it. Please do not send unpublished manuscript files, research participant records, student assessments, payment information or other confidential attachments through this temporary contact channel. A message requesting collaboration is not confirmation of a reviewer appointment, journal submission, editorial decision or publication. The authorised public pages will explain any future changes to manuscript intake or contact arrangements after the corresponding technical and governance checks are complete.
