Editorial Board

The international editorial board is being constituted. No scholar will be listed until that individual has explicitly accepted an editorial role. This page will therefore remain intentionally incomplete rather than presenting unconfirmed affiliations or names.

Conflict management

Editorial decisions involving the Editor-in-Chief’s own manuscripts, close collaborators or other material conflicts will be delegated to an independent handling editor once the board is formally constituted.
