Fees & funding

Fees must be transparent, post-review and never confused with acceptance.

The journal will not charge a submission fee. Any future Article Processing Charge (APC), waiver, institutional payment arrangement or founding-issue exemption will be stated publicly before submissions open.

Founding recommendation

The founding issue should be fee-waived. This allows the journal to establish editorial standards, recruit strong work and demonstrate that acceptance is determined by scholarship rather than payment.

Payment timing

If an APC is introduced later, payment should be requested only after editorial acceptance. DOI registration will not be sold as an optional add-on; every eligible published research article will receive the same identifier policy.

Payment records

The production platform will retain invoice number, manuscript ID, payer, amount, currency, payment status, waiver status, transaction reference and reconciliation date. Card or banking credentials will never be stored in the journal database.
