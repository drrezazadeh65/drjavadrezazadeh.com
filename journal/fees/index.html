Fees & funding

Fees must be transparent, post-review and never confused with acceptance.

The journal will not charge a submission fee. Any future Article Processing Charge (APC), waiver, institutional payment arrangement or founding-issue exemption will be stated publicly before submissions open.

Founding recommendation

The founding issue should be fee-waived. This allows the journal to establish editorial standards, recruit strong work and demonstrate that acceptance is determined by scholarship rather than payment.

Payment timing

If an APC is introduced later, payment should be requested only after editorial acceptance. DOI registration will not be sold as an optional add-on; every eligible published research article will receive the same identifier policy.

Payment records

The production platform will retain invoice number, manuscript ID, payer, amount, currency, payment status, waiver status, transaction reference and reconciliation date. Card or banking credentials will never be stored in the journal database.

Access model and founding safeguards

The founding editorial policy proposes free access for readers and no fee for manuscript submission. The inaugural issue is intended to be fee-waived while the journal establishes its review and production systems. This principle aims to separate scholarly judgement from the financial position of an author and to make the first publishing decisions accountable to editorial quality.

Should article-processing charges be considered later, the journal must disclose the exact amount, currency, any taxes, waiver eligibility, refund conditions and timing before accepting submissions. Payments must never influence peer-review assignment, handling-editor decisions or the speed of acceptance. A valid DOI, where assigned through an authorised legal registration route, is not an optional paid marker of academic prestige.

Licensing and copyright

Before the first article is released, authors will be given explicit information on the retention or transfer of rights, open-access licensing, reuse permissions and third-party content. The journal's intended access model should remain compatible with transparent, non-exclusive scholarly dissemination; detailed licence choices will be publicly approved before manuscript intake is opened.

Current reality: no formal manuscript-submission or article-processing payment is being accepted for JHELA. The journal's submission process remains in preparation.
