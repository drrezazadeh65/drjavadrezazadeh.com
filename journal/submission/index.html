Submission architecture

Submit only when the editorial platform opens.

The journal will use a role-based editorial management system rather than email-only manuscript handling. Author registration, manuscript submission, revision, peer review, copyediting, production and publication will be managed through the journal platform.

Planned submission sequence

Author account → ORCID and affiliation details → manuscript metadata → author declarations → manuscript files → editorial screening → peer review → decision → revision where required → acceptance → publication-fee decision or waiver → production → DOI registration → publication.

Current status

Formal submissions are not yet open. No manuscript should be sent as a paid submission during the founding stage. Once OJS is deployed on the independent journal domain, this page will route authors directly into the submission workflow.
