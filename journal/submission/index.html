Submission architecture

Submit only when the editorial platform opens.

The journal will use a role-based editorial management system rather than email-only manuscript handling. Author registration, manuscript submission, revision, peer review, copyediting, production and publication will be managed through the journal platform.

Formal submissionClosedDo not send a paid or formal manuscript submission yet.
PlatformOJS plannedSubmission opens only through the journal workflow.
FeesNo submission feeNo founding-stage payment creates editorial priority.
DecisionEditorially independentPayment and acceptance remain separate.

Planned submission sequence

01AccountAuthor identity, affiliation and ORCID where available
02MetadataTitle, abstract, keywords and declarations
03ScreeningScope, integrity and editorial checks
04ReviewIndependent peer review where applicable
05DecisionRevision, reject or accept with editorial record
06ProductionCopyedit, metadata, DOI and publication

Current status

Formal submissions are not yet open. No manuscript should be sent as a paid submission during the founding stage. Once OJS is deployed on the independent journal domain, this page will route authors directly into the submission workflow.
