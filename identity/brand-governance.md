- Do not use unverified AI-rendered Persian lettering for official documents; typeset the approved name as actual text or outlined reviewed glyphs.
- Keep SVGs semantic, with title/desc, and avoid external resources or executable SVG content.
- No unapproved qualifications, certification claims, signatures, payment data, seals or QR codes.
- Final deployment requires visual approval, source provenance and integration QA.
