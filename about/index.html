Educational Philosophy

Education is not merely the transfer of knowledge; it is the awakening of the human being.

My educational philosophy begins from a simple but demanding conviction: education must not lose the human being while pursuing information, achievement or measurement. Knowledge remains incomplete when it does not contribute to character; talent remains only a possibility when it is separated from effort; and counselling fails when it replaces a learner’s decision instead of helping that learner learn how to decide.

Adapted from the book «کشف مسیر طلایی استعداد» by Dr. Javad Rezazadeh Yazdeli.
