About

Dr. Javad Rezazadeh Yazdeli

University lecturer, education researcher and educational consultant working across English-language education, applied linguistics, assessment, educational materials, talent identification and academic guidance.

Academic background

Dr. Javad Rezazadeh Yazdeli completed his doctorate at Arak University in 2026. His dissertation examined the cultural and pedagogical fit of the Top Notch series in Iranian EFL contexts through a mixed-methods design. He previously completed an M.A. in English Language Teaching at the University of Tehran and a B.A. in English Language and Literature at Islamic Azad University, Qom.

His work has developed at the intersection of university teaching, language education, educational research and professional consultation. This combination informs an approach in which educational decisions are treated as contextual judgements rather than as mechanical applications of a single test or metric.

Teaching and professional practice

His teaching experience extends across higher-education institutions in Iran, including current university teaching at Tehran University of Medical Sciences and previous teaching roles at the University of Kashan, Payame Noor University Kashan and other higher-education institutions. Alongside teaching, his professional work has included educational planning, academic guidance, field-of-study selection, talent identification and communication with learners and families.

Research direction

Current interests include applied linguistics, English-language education, higher education, language assessment and validity, curriculum and materials evaluation, vocabulary and fluency, technology-enhanced learning, AI in education and human-centred approaches to assessment. Emerging programmes include Humanability and TESTLY, alongside continuing work on teacher humanization and talent development.
