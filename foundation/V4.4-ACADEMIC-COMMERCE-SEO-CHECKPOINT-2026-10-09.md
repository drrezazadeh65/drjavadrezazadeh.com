## Communication and customer support governance

Account verification and recovery are **EMAIL-ONLY**; phone numbers may serve operational contact needs but must not authenticate anyone. Public pages and the checkout JavaScript no longer expose the Eitaa support link. The 18 public assets/js scripts were individually inspected for direct Eitaa link/handle leakage. Access to Eitaa support must be restricted to verified, eligible student customers; removing a link does not prevent access to a historically public account and cannot by itself enforce exclusivity.

The server-only paid-student-support policy checks for server-validated email sessions, student role, order ownership, paid payment intent, matching verified payment, confirmed refund-ledger status, applicable active entitlement, start/end dates and refund revocation. Nineteen local Node.js unit tests passed; the relevant CI test is included in the release workflow. **No production endpoint or verified post-purchase disclosure mechanism is claimed to be active yet.** A controlled invitation mechanism and/or replacement private support channel may still be required because the old contact link was publicly known.

## Checkout, privacy and operational status

The previous checkout script requested an optional customer email while the existing commerce API ignored customerEmail and did not persist it with the order. This misleading and unnecessary collection was removed pending a proper server-side email-backed order, receipt and account workflow. Until such a workflow is implemented, do not claim that checkout emails, invoices or durable email-linked customer records are sent or created.

The standard/VIP checkout buttons now default to disabled while the client checks /commerce/health. The commerce implementation and single-file worker candidate were updated to report separate readiness for services, VIP and books, derived from server-side configuration and not from catalogue prices. The frontend checks this readiness where reported, and displays a clear unavailable state otherwise. **Deployment of the updated Cloudflare worker and live gateway readiness have not been independently confirmed.** An earlier live payment demo or Vercel READY status must not be interpreted as proof that service purchases, identity or receipts work end-to-end.

5. Independently crawl the public domain on desktop and mobile and verify all changed routes, canonical URLs, images, CSS, JSON-LD, sitemaps, email links and language-switcher pathways. External browser access was unavailable in this run.
6. Obtain evidence of a successfully verified end-to-end low-value payment and an email-linked receipt in a properly configured environment before authorising a full paid-service release.

No public academic claims, prices, payment credentials or previously released route paths were deliberately changed during this checkpoint. Until the remaining controls are verified, treat the result as **a significantly improved v4.4 candidate, not final release certification**.
