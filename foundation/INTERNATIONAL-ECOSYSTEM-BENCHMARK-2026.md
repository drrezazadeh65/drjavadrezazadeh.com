# International Ecosystem Benchmark — 2026

**Purpose:** extract durable product/UX/architecture lessons from established international education and student-development platforms without copying proprietary content or importing their scientific claims.

## Benchmarks reviewed

### Gallup — CliftonStrengths for Students
Observed product pattern: a student-specific journey from assessment to personalized report to applied development, with role-specific student framing, sample-report visibility and action-oriented interpretation.

**Adopted design lesson:** make the journey understandable before purchase or assessment, show what a future report/workflow contains, and convert interpretation into concrete next actions.  
**Not adopted:** Golden Talent does not inherit Gallup's construct model, scoring, themes or validity claims.

### YouScience — Aptitude & Career Discovery / Brightpath
Observed product pattern: flexible pacing, resumeable assessment activity, student/educator/family use cases, pathway exploration and connected planning dashboards.

**Adopted design lesson:** support pause/resume, visible progress, role-specific surfaces and continuity from evidence to planning.  
**Not adopted:** Golden Talent does not import aptitude determinism, proprietary algorithms or career-match claims.

### My Next Move / O*NET
Observed product pattern: transparent career-reference exploration by interests, keywords and industry, with explicit source/citation information.

**Adopted architecture lesson:** external career or education taxonomies should enter through versioned, licensed, jurisdiction-aware adapters with provenance. Reference data must remain distinct from a judgement about a person.

## Design target created from the benchmark

The ecosystem should provide a clearer end-to-end journey than a conventional academic website:

**Discover → Understand → Contribute Evidence → Review → Explore Pathways → Try Real Experiences → Reassess**

The system must remain:
- bilingual and jurisdiction-aware;
- mobile-first and resumeable;
- role-aware for student, parent, teacher and adviser;
- transparent about evidence and uncertainty;
- non-deterministic;
- extensible to multiple career/qualification taxonomies;
- explicit about what is public information versus private personal evidence.
