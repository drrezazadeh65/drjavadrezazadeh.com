# Master Website Evolution Alignment — v1

**Date:** 2026-10-06  
**Source of direction:** MASTER WEBSITE EVOLUTION COMMAND supplied by the project owner.  
**Rule:** preserve all healthy existing work; evolve incrementally and do not reopen frozen safety, SEO, privacy or scientific constraints without evidence.

## 1. North-star interpretation

drjavadrezazadeh.com is no longer treated as a personal/CV website. It is an **Authority + Education + Intelligence + Revenue Ecosystem** with one public authority layer and multiple connected engines.

The product architecture is organised around three defensible moats:

1. **Brand moat** — Dr. Javad Rezazadeh's verified academic identity, work, publications, educational judgement and human relationship.
2. **Product & data moat** — Golden Talent, longitudinal student evidence, methodology, professional review and future decision intelligence.
3. **Discovery moat** — bilingual topical/entity authority, SEO/GEO, structured data, citation readiness, internal links and earned academic authority.

A new feature should normally strengthen at least one moat and should pass the product filter: authority, revenue/conversion, accumulated user value/data, long-term moat, material UX value, and fit with the scientific/professional identity.

## 2. Product architecture

### Authority Engine
Public, index-governed surfaces:
- About / Academic Profile / Public CV
- Research / Publications / Books / Teaching
- Academic Engagements / Collaboration / Speaking & Invitations
- Knowledge Hub
- Publisher and JHELA, each with separate governance

### Revenue Engine
Public discovery plus private fulfilment:
- Academic and researcher services
- Student and parent consultation
- Teacher mentoring / professional development
- Institutional consulting / training / speaking
- Digital products and books
- Human-reviewed reports
- Later recurring coaching/programmes

Required sequence:

**Service discovery → scoped request → secure intake → booking → verified payment where applicable → account → delivery → follow-up**

No browser state may become payment, identity, entitlement or professional-decision authority.

### Golden Talent Engine
Positioning:

**Educational Development & Decision Intelligence Platform**

Core product journey:

**Discover → Assess → Plan → Coach → Track → Predict → Decide → Grow**

The core remains country-agnostic. Iran, IB, A-Level, AP, GPA-based and other pathways are adapters/modules, never the identity of the core.

### Intelligence Layer
AI is not a decorative chatbot. The future layer may support document analysis, trend detection, adaptive planning, anomaly detection, report drafting, recommendation explanation and pathway simulation, but consequential recommendations remain **AI-assisted + evidence-based + human-supervised**.

## 3. Information architecture

### Four public audience gates
1. **Students & Parents** — talent, study, coaching, pathways
2. **Researchers & Academics** — research, editing, publication, academic career
3. **Teachers & Educators** — development, assessment, mentoring
4. **Institutions** — consulting, training, research, speaking

The homepage and Services hub should use these gates without turning the global navigation into a catalogue.

### Language strategy
Persian and English share infrastructure and entity identity but not mechanical content mirroring.

Persian emphasises students, parents, study, coaching, talent, Iran educational pathways and academic services for Iranian researchers.

English emphasises international authority, research, publications, educational thought, assessment, teacher development, academic consulting, speaking, institutional consulting and future international pathways.

## 4. Dashboard architecture

Role-specific application experience is the rule.

### Student
My Path, My Talent, My Progress, Study Plan, Goals, Reports, Assessments, Exams, Consultations, University Options, Career Exploration.

Primary questions visible at a glance:
- Where am I now?
- Where am I going?
- Am I improving?
- What should I do next?
- What needs attention?

### Parent
Child progress, periodic reports, programme adherence, goals, sessions, adviser recommendations, parent consultation, joint sessions and billing — subject to relationship and privacy scope.

### Teacher
Assigned-student scope, learning evidence, observations and teaching-relevant feedback only.

### Adviser/Counsellor
Assigned cases, triage, evidence review, professional notes, human approval, follow-up and audit trail.

### Researcher / Consultation Client / Institution / Admin
Architecture is reserved and must remain role-specific rather than reusing the student dashboard.

## 5. Data architecture

The existing model already provides strong foundations for identity, relationships, consent, assessment provenance, educational records, consultation, commerce, Golden Talent, research and audit.

Required evolution:
- country-agnostic Student Profile
- longitudinal observations/events rather than current-state-only storage
- academic-performance time series
- goals and decision history
- study-plan revisions
- consultation/coaching history
- saved pathway options
- country-specific educational reference adapters with source/version/provenance
- explainable recommendation records
- AI output provenance and human-review state

Annual or jurisdiction-specific educational data must never be stored without source, version, effective date and provenance.
