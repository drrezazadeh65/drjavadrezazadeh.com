## Changes committed to main
1. The 21 standard service pages no longer advertise an unverified Schema.org Offer or InStock availability. Each Service schema now has its canonical service URL and own illustration URL. Pages explicitly explain that publishing a price does not mean that payment or booking is operational.
1a. All 21 standard service pages now contain a distinct, substantive Persian explanation of their professional fee and scope, without promises of exam rank, admission, publication or employment. The SEO gate checks for the presence of the fee-rationale section.
2. Six VIP detail pages retain their own illustrations and Service/BreadcrumbList schema, without unverified Offer markup. The 21+6 service catalogue is preserved.
3. The SEO service gate now enforces canonical URLs, per-service schema images, no unverified offers, honest booking disclosures and academic provenance.
4. The pre-gateway commerce schema audit now traverses nested JSON-LD structures, closing the prior blind spot where Service.offers could evade a top-level-only scan.
5. The service/VIP checkout cards show their own illustrations; standard services also show their fit statement. Missing or malformed service identifiers cannot be used to build arbitrary image URLs.
6. The Persian service checkout and VIP catalogue no longer claim that an unverified payment flow is live. The service checkout has a keyboard skip link and visible focus treatment.
7. Book cart and checkout buttons now default to disabled, and only enable after the live Cloudflare commerce health endpoint explicitly reports checkout=true AND capabilities.books=true. A legacy generic checkout=true without a books capability is insufficient.
8. Dynamic book catalogue fields inserted into HTML are escaped, and cover paths are restricted to the owned /assets/images/books/ directory. The original book covers, titles, prices and identifiers are preserved.
9. Source regression assertions and two Playwright tests were added for disabled payment on a legacy health response and the 27 illustrated, disabled service checkout cards.
