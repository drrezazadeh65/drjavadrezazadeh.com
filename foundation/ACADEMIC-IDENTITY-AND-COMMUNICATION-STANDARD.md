# Academic Identity and Communication Standard — v4.4

**Status:** Binding editorial and product-design policy for the bilingual public website.  
**Identity:** Dr. Javad Rezazadeh Yazdeli / دکتر جواد رضازاده یزدلی.  
**Scope:** Public homepages, scholarly pages, educational services, service-detail pages, checkout narratives, student journeys, customer support and SEO.

## 1. Primary institutional voice

This website represents the scholarly and professional work of a university lecturer, education researcher and author. Its commercial services are practical applications of this work rather than a separate, dominant retail identity. Accordingly, the first level of the information architecture should make the academic record discoverable: education, research, teaching, publications, academic engagements and publicly authorised projects. Individual consultations, educational guidance, talent assessment and paid professional services should be presented as context-sensitive activities arising from this record.

The Persian and English sites must retain the full personal name consistently. Degree titles and research fields must remain distinct: the public academic profile describes a PhD in Education and an M.A. in English Language Teaching. Do not invent institutional appointments, publication counts, peer-review status, journal indexing, endorsements, qualifications or current affiliations. Where a status is pending or time-sensitive, it should be labelled accurately rather than silently converted into a current credential.

## 2. Evidence before conversion

A visitor considering any paid service should be able to understand its intended audience, prerequisites, methodological rationale, expected deliverable, timing, price, exclusions and professional limitations. Service pages must provide navigable connections to the public academic profile, research fields and publication record so that professional claims can be evaluated. Research-backed guidance is not a promise of admission, examination rank, clinical diagnosis, journal acceptance or employment.

The current programme contains **21 standard service records and six separate VIP listings: 27 defined offers in total**. The VIP entries have distinct pages and illustrations, but their proposed prices and conditional booking arrangements must not be misrepresented as fully operational payment and fulfilment. Preserve this differentiation while auditing all 27 for scope, images, professional justification, pricing, privacy and transaction readiness. Future additions require approved specifications and a corresponding SEO review.

## 3. Communication and entitlement boundaries

Email is the sole channel for account registration, activation, login identification, recovery and general academic/customer correspondence. A phone number may be collected for agreed operational contact but **must not** authenticate an account, initiate password recovery, or confer access. Do not introduce SMS or WhatsApp verification.

An Eitaa support channel may be used solely for eligible students who have purchased an applicable service and whose entitlement has been verified. The Eitaa ID or join link must not appear in publicly indexable marketing, service or contact pages. An authenticated dashboard should disclose access only after a server-side confirmation of the applicable paid entitlement; merely hiding an HTML link is not access control. Expiry, cancellation, refunds and revocation should be reflected in the entitlement checks. Before that mechanism has been independently verified, describe the policy without claiming that automatic customer-only access is already operational.

## 4. Academic publication, privacy and SEO

Published work, accepted work, research in development and unpublished/embargoed projects must not be conflated. Unreleased research should remain inaccessible to public pages, public JSON, sitemaps and search snippets. The frozen bilingual SEO standard and existing canonical URLs remain binding; do not rename or discard released paths, break existing links or silently alter hreflang mappings. Scholarly provenance, genuine images and relevant contextual cross-links take priority over keyword repetition or exaggerated marketing language.

## 5. Release checks

For any service-related release, verify that:
- All indexable service pages contain the complete academic name and links to public scholarly evidence.
- No public service page exposes Eitaa access information or presents it as a pre-purchase contact channel.
- Identity-policy data retains email verification and email recovery, without telephone authentication.
- The service catalogue, indexed detail pages, prices, images, canonical URLs, structured data and sitemaps remain mutually consistent.
- Customer-only support access is marked operational **only after** tested server-side purchase verification and entitlement enforcement.

The automated service SEO gate implements the available static checks. It does not substitute for authentication, gateway, deployment or real-customer end-to-end tests.
