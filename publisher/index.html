Independent by design

Rezazadeh Foundation Press is currently independent of any university, commercial publisher or academic society. Its editorial and publishing decisions are therefore not represented as institutional decisions of any university with which its founder may teach, research or collaborate.

The use of the word Foundation identifies the publishing initiative and intellectual platform at this founding stage; no claim of charitable or statutory foundation status is made unless and until such status is formally established under applicable law.

Journal programme

The first scholarly journal under development is the Journal of Human-Centred Education, Learning and Assessment (JHELA). The journal is being built with independent editorial governance, transparent peer review, publication ethics, persistent identifiers and an open call for qualified reviewers and founding editorial collaborators.
